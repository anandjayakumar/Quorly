# Quorly — Private Browser Writing App & Markdown Editor

> A private, distraction-free browser writing app and Markdown editor that runs entirely in your browser — and keeps working offline. No account, no sign-up, no upload, no ads, no cookies. Your draft never leaves your device.

- Editor: <https://quorly.pages.dev/app/>
- Privacy: <https://quorly.pages.dev/privacy/>
- Canonical page: <https://quorly.pages.dev/>

## A minimalist browser writing app built for focus and privacy

Everything you need to finish a draft. Nothing you don't.

### Write without friction

Rich text with headings, bold and italic, lists, quotes, links, inline code and aligned text — plus a floating toolbar that appears right where your cursor is.

### Focus Mode

Press `Esc` to clear the interface down to the page and your sentence. Press it again to come back. The tool disappears; the writing doesn't.

### Images, tables, code

Paste or drop an image, insert a table, drop in a divider, or add a code block with syntax-highlighted monospace text.

### Markdown and PDF export

One click to Markdown for your notes app or static-site generator, one click to PDF, one click to plain text or a `.quorly` package that keeps your images, or save straight to disk with the File System Access API.

### Your documents, kept local

Every document you write is saved in this browser and listed in Recents — search them, reopen them, delete them. Nothing is uploaded, and nothing needs an account.

### Yours to keep

Light and dark themes, a choice of reading typefaces, adjustable text size and column width. Autosave keeps everything in your browser between visits.

## Questions

### Is Quorly really free?

Yes. The editor runs entirely in your browser, so it costs almost nothing to serve. There is no account, no trial and no paywall on the writing features.

### Where are my documents stored?

In this browser, on this device. The open document lives in local storage and the documents you have parked live in the browser's own database. Use **Export** to keep a permanent copy: `.quorly` keeps the document with its images, and Markdown, PDF or plain text are one click away.

### Do I need to sign up or log in?

No. There is no account system at all. Open the editor and start writing.

### Can I export my writing?

Yes — export to Markdown, PDF, plain text or a `.quorly` package (the document plus its images), or write the file straight to disk with your operating system's file picker.

### Does it work offline?

Once the page has loaded, yes. Everything after that — editing, formatting, exporting — happens locally.

### What about images and tables?

You can paste or drop images, and insert tables, dividers and code blocks. Images are embedded directly in the document.
