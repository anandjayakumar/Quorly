Privacy

Your writing stays yours

Quorly is a private writing app that runs entirely in your browser. Your documents, titles, images, and drafts stay on your device. We never receive or store your writing on our servers.

Your documents and settings are saved in your browser, so clearing your browser's site data may permanently delete them. Export important documents to keep a backup.

Anonymous analytics

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand general website traffic and performance. It collects anonymous, aggregate information such as page visits, approximate country, browser type, and page-load performance.

Quorly also counts basic usage events, such as opening the editor, starting to write, and exporting a document. These counts do not include your writing, document titles, or other document content.

Learn more in Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.

No accounts or tracking

Quorly requires no account, sign-up, or email address. We don't use cookies for tracking, and we never upload your documents.

Contact

For questions, feedback, or security concerns, contact hello@quorly.dev. Contact is provided on a best-effort basis, and a response is not guaranteed.
