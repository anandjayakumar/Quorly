Privacy

Effective 16 October 2026

The short version

Quorly is a writing app that runs entirely in your browser. We do not receive your documents, your titles, your images, your keystrokes or your file names. There is no server that stores your writing. The only thing we measure is anonymous page visits.

What never leaves your device

  • Your writing. Document titles and bodies, including embedded images, are stored in this browser on your own machine — the open document in local storage, the documents you have parked in the browser's own database.
  • Your versions. Local snapshots of earlier drafts are kept in the browser's own database, never sent anywhere.
  • Your files. When you export, the file is written directly to your disk through the browser's file picker. It is never uploaded.

What we do measure

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics for anonymous visit counts. It is cookieless and does not use fingerprinting or cross-site identifiers. It reports aggregate information such as:

  • Page URL and referring site
  • Coarse location (country) and browser, operating system and device type
  • Page-load performance metrics (Core Web Vitals)

It does not record what you type, and we never send document content to it. This aggregate data is handled by Cloudflare under their privacy policy.

What we store on your device

Quorly keeps the open document and your settings in this browser's local storage (keys such as doc_title_v13, doc_body_v13 and doc_title_image_v13), and keeps the documents you have parked (with their earlier snapshots) in the browser's own database. They never leave your device, and clearing your browser's site data deletes them permanently — so export anything important.

No accounts, but you can reach us

Quorly has no sign-up, no login and no newsletter. We never ask for your email address, and your draft never leaves your device. If you want to reach us — to report a problem, report a security issue, or ask a question — write to hello@quorly.us.ci. That is a best-effort contact with no SLA: nobody is obligated to reply, and there is no support desk.
